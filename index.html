AI Fatigue Classification Parameters

Configured safety boundaries for automated worker fatigue triggers

Heart Rate Threshold 76.0 – 95.0 bpm

Values exceeding 95 bpm or falling below 76 bpm under workload flag cardiovascular stress.

Blink Rate Threshold (Ocular Fatigue) 16.0 – 25.0 /min

Measured via infrared blink sensor inside helmet brim. Low blink rate indicates microsleep risk.

Core Body Temperature Threshold 36.5 – 37.2 °C

Contact forehead thermopile sensor. >37.3 °C signals early heat exhaustion.

Head Acceleration Threshold (Imbalance/Slip) 0.55 – 0.88 g

6-axis IMU on helmet suspension. Sudden spikes or erratic instability trigger alerts.
